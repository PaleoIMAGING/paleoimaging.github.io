# About

<p class="lead">PaleoIMAGING is a Working Group of <a href="https://pastglobalchanges.org/science/current-wg/paleoimaging">PAGES</a> (Past Global Changes) for the community that uses imaging and analytical scanning techniques on paleoenvironmental archives.</p>

The techniques covered include hyperspectral imaging, XRF core scanning, micro-XRF and micro-CT, applied to archives such as sediment cores. The group brings together laboratories and researchers who run these methods so that measurements can be compared, documented and shared.

## Objectives

- Agree on **standard materials** that laboratories can measure and compare.
- Run **interlaboratory comparisons** and move towards harmonised acquisition.
- Describe **best practices** for acquisition, processing and data quality.
- Support **datasets** that are well documented, interoperable and reusable.

Work is organised in four [Working Groups]({{ '/working-groups/' | relative_url }}) and coordinated by the [Core Group]({{ '/core-group/' | relative_url }}). To follow or take part, [join the mailing list](https://listserv.unibe.ch/mailman/listinfo/paleoimaging.pages).
