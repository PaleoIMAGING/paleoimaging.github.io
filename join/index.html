Join the PaleoIMAGING Community

Stay connected with PaleoIMAGING through our mailing list. Receive announcements about meetings, workshops, collaborative activities and opportunities to participate.

About the mailing list

The mailing list is the community’s announcement and communication channel. It is the main way we share meeting invitations, workshop news and calls for participation with researchers and technicians who use imaging and analytical scanning techniques on paleoenvironmental archives.

How to subscribe

Send an empty email from the address you wish to subscribe. No subject or message is required.

Subscribe by email

You may receive an email asking you to confirm your subscription. If you do not see it, please check your spam folder.
