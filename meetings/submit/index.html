Propose a meeting

Use this form to propose a Working Group meeting, workshop or community event for the PaleoIMAGING calendar. Every proposal is reviewed and approved by administrators before it is published. Nothing appears on the calendar until then.

{%- if live %}

Before you submit: this is public

  • When you submit, the details of your proposal are placed in a public GitHub pull request in the PaleoIMAGING website repository, where anyone can read them before an administrator approves it. If it is not approved, the pull request is closed but may remain visible in GitHub’s history.
  • Once approved, the meeting appears on the public calendar and in the downloadable calendar feed, including the public meeting link, if you give one.
  • Only enter information you are happy to see published. Never enter private join links, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal contact details. Organizer names and affiliations are shown publicly.
  • To protect the form from spam, a Cloudflare Turnstile check runs when you submit. Cloudflare processes technical data from your browser for this check under its privacy policy. We keep only a one-way scrambled form of your network address, temporarily, to limit repeated submissions; we do not store it in readable form.
